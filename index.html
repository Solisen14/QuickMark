A note on balanced assessment

Multiple-choice questions can be a valuable way to assess student learning. They offer efficiency, consistency, and useful insights into what students know and understand.

But no single assessment format tells the whole story.

Students demonstrate learning in different ways. Depending on the learning goals, consider balancing multiple-choice questions with other opportunities for students to demonstrate their knowledge, skills, thinking, and understanding.

Other ways to gather evidence of learning

  • ◉ Conversations & discussions
  • ✎ Written & creative responses
  • ▣ Presentations & demonstrations
  • ◎ Observations & practical tasks

QuickMark is designed to make multiple-choice marking easier, not to replace the professional judgment involved in assessing student learning.
